# Notice to operators: vessels in class

Issued by the Authority to operators under the Coastal Craft (Voyage and
Returns) Regulations 2021.

1. This notice applies to a vessel entered with a classification society the
   Authority recognises (a classed vessel).
2. A vessel is classed from the date the society enters it until the entry
   lapses or is withdrawn.
3. The engines of a classed vessel are overhauled on the dates the society
   sets, in place of reg. 11 and Schedule 2.
4. A classed vessel is surveyed on the date the society sets, in place of
   the intervals in reg. 17.
5. The society issues a status report for the vessel each season, giving the
   date each engine is next due for overhaul and the date of the next
   survey.
6. The operating return for a classed vessel states the dates in the latest
   status report.
7. Where no status report covers a season in which the vessel was classed,
   the operator may not rely on class for that season.
8. Nothing in this notice changes the levy under regs. 24 to 26.
9. Until the society first surveys the vessel, reg. 17 continues to apply.
