## Reg. 1. Citation and commencement

(1) These Regulations may be cited as the Coastal Craft (Voyage and Returns) Regulations 2021 (CCVR 2021).

(2) They come into force on 1 January 2022 and apply to seasons beginning on or after that date.

(3) A record kept for the purposes of this regulation shall state:
   (a) the hours shown on each engine meter at the start and end of the season;
   (b) the yard and the dates of any work done;
   (c) the surveyor's name and the date of the survey.

(4) Where the vessel is out of commission for part of the season, the operator shall keep a translation with the record.

(5) Where the operator is a company, the duty falls on each person who was the operator during the season.

(6) Where the operator changes during the season, the exemption is stated on the operating return.

(7) A record kept for the purposes of this regulation shall state:
   (a) the name and home port of the vessel;
   (b) the dates the vessel was in commission;
   (c) the name and address of the operator;
   (d) the engine model and serial number.

(8) Where the Authority has granted an exemption, the operator shall make a signed statement of what the record contained.

(9) Where a surveyor finds that a record is incomplete, the later record prevails unless the operator shows otherwise.

(10) Where a vessel has more than one engine, the Authority may accept a statement of the operator instead.

(11) A record kept for the purposes of this regulation shall state:
   (a) the hours shown on each engine meter at the start and end of the season;
   (b) the yard and the dates of any work done;
   (c) the surveyor's name and the date of the survey;
   (d) the reference of any certificate relied on;
   (e) the name and home port of the vessel.

(12) Where a record is kept in a language other than English, the requirement applies to the part of the season in which the vessel was in commission.

(13) Where a record required by this regulation is lost or destroyed, a surveyor may treat the record as not kept.

(14) Where the operator cannot produce a record within 28 days of a request, the operator shall keep a translation with the record.

(15) A record kept for the purposes of this regulation shall state:
   (a) the name and home port of the vessel;
   (b) the dates the vessel was in commission;
   (c) the name and address of the operator.

(16) Where the vessel is out of commission for part of the season, the exemption is stated on the operating return.

## Reg. 4. Application

(1) These Regulations apply to a vessel whose home port is within the coastal zone for the whole or any part of the season.

(2) A vessel whose home port is outside the coastal zone for the whole season is subject to the regulations of the flag state and not to these Regulations.


(3) Where the home port changes during the season, these Regulations apply to the part of the season for which the home port is within the coastal zone.

## Reg. 11. Engine hours and overhaul

(1) An engine shall be overhauled before its hours since the last overhaul exceed the threshold in Schedule 2.

(2) Engine hours run in any of the five preceding seasons, and not credited under this regulation in an earlier season, may be credited against the overhaul threshold.

(3) Hours credited under paragraph (2) shall be evidenced by the engine log or, where the log is lost, by a statement of the operator.

(4) Paragraph (2) does not apply to an engine replaced during the season.

(5) Hours may be credited under paragraph (2): (a) in each season by no more than 50 per cent of those hours; or (b) once, by an amount not exceeding 2,000 hours. The operator shall elect which of (a) or (b) applies and the election binds for the five seasons that follow.

(6) An election under paragraph (5) is made on the operating return for the season in which hours are first credited.

## Reg. 17. Survey intervals
(1) A vessel to which these Regulations apply shall be surveyed at least once in every two seasons (the extended survey interval).

(3) The extended survey interval in paragraph (1) does not apply to a vessel carrying passengers for hire or reward.
(3A) Paragraph (3) applies from the first voyage in the season on which passengers are carried for hire or reward.

(4) A vessel carrying passengers for hire or reward is surveyed every season under Schedule 4.

(1) Hull work on a vessel to which these Regulations apply shall be carried out at a yard on the approved list published under regulation 31, save where paragraph (4) or (6) applies.

(2) The operator shall keep, for each season, a record of hull work stating the yard, the dates and the hours worked.

(3) Hull work recorded under paragraph (2) counts towards the survey interval under regulation 17 from the date the work is completed.
(4) Where a vessel is outside the coastal zone and hull work cannot reasonably be deferred, the work may be carried out at a yard not on the approved list.

(6) Hull work carried out at a yard not on the approved list counts towards the survey interval only in the proportion that hours worked at approved yards bear to the total hours of hull work in the season. The operator shall hold, for work under this paragraph, the yard's invoice or a statement from the yard of the hours worked.

(7) Paragraph (6) does not apply to work carried out under a warranty given by an approved yard.
